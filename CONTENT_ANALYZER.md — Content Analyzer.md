# Content Analyzer

## Collect

- visible text
- word count
- character count
- paragraphs
- language
- content fingerprint

## Duplicate Detection

Support:

- exact duplicate
- near duplicate

Use deterministic algorithms.

Do not use AI embeddings.

## Terminology

Prefer:

`Low text-content volume`

instead of:

`Bad content`

The crawler reports measurable characteristics, not subjective editorial quality.
