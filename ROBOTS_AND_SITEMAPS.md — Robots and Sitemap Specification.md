# Robots and Sitemap

## robots.txt

Fetch dynamically.

Parse:

- User-agent
- Allow
- Disallow
- Sitemap

Default:

Respect robots.txt.

## Sitemap

Detect:

- robots sitemap declarations
- `/sitemap.xml`
- sitemap index

Parse:

- loc
- lastmod
- changefreq
- priority

## Comparison

Calculate:

- sitemap URLs
- crawled URLs
- sitemap-only URLs
- crawl-only URLs
- broken sitemap URLs
- redirected sitemap URLs

Never assume sitemap completeness.
